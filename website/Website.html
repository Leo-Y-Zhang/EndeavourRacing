Who We Are

ENGINEERS.
NOT PASSENGERS.

Endeavour Racing is a team of six students from Harrow School. We built our car from the ground up — designing, testing, arguing over details, scrapping ideas, and starting again until we got it right.

There are no passengers here. Every person on this team thinks, designs, debates, and delivers. From the earliest design sketches to the final race day lap, every member plays a critical role.

We compete in the Development Class at STEM Racing Nationals 2026 — six determined students from Harrow School who design, debate, build, and deliver together.

Our Story

WHY ENDEAVOUR?

OUTTHINK✦ OUTBUILD✦ OUTRACE✦ ENDEAVOUR RACING✦ OUTTHINK✦ OUTBUILD✦ OUTRACE✦ ENDEAVOUR RACING✦ OUTTHINK✦ OUTBUILD✦ OUTRACE✦ ENDEAVOUR RACING✦

To endeavour means to keep going even when you're not sure it's going to work out. That felt right for us. Engineering a race car as a group of students is not easy — things break, simulations don't behave, and deadlines come fast.

Every time we hit a problem, we don't step back. We test a new approach, run another simulation, rethink the design, and go again. That attitude is baked into the name we chose.

HMS Endeavour carried Captain Cook on a voyage into the unknown, charting territory no one had mapped before. The Space Shuttle Endeavour pushed human achievement beyond the atmosphere. Both were attached to people pushing into territory they'd never been before.

That is what we are trying to do — in our own way, on our own scale.
